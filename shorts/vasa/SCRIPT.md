## Voice-over (≈247 words → ≈67 s at 3.7 w/s)

**HOOK**
> In sixteen twenty-eight, Sweden built the mightiest warship in Europe. It sank before it left the harbour.

**SETTING**
> Stockholm. The king wanted a ship that would terrify his enemies. So the Vasa got sixty-four bronze cannons, on not one, but two gun decks.
> The shipbuilder had never built a ship with two gun decks.

**ACTION (escalation)**
> Before the launch, the admiral ordered a test. Thirty sailors, running from one side of the deck to the other.
> First run, she rocked. Second run, she rolled. Third run, the admiral stopped them, before the ship fell over.
> He said, "If only His Majesty were at home." And launched it anyway.

**SNAP**
> The tenth of August. Gun ports open for the farewell salute. Thirteen hundred metres out, one gust of wind leaned her over, and the sea came in through the gun ports.

**DEADPAN DOUBLE**
> Luckily, the harbour was only thirty-two metres deep.
> Unluckily, that left the tops of her masts sticking out of the water, for everyone to see.

**EXPLANATION**
> Ever stood up in a rowing boat? Weight up high wants to tip. Weight down low pulls you back. The Vasa was all cannon on top, and almost nothing underneath.

**CONSEQUENCE**
> At the inquiry, nobody was punished. The builder had died the year before. And the man who approved the design… was the king.

**CALLBACK TWIST**
> Three hundred and thirty-three years later, they lifted her out. Now over a million people a year come to see her.
> And just to be safe, none of them are allowed on deck.
