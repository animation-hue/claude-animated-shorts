## Lessons (fiction / vehicle cast)
- Vehicle characters: headlights = eyes, a light bumper plate = mouth (dark ink on a black body vanishes); lids in the
  roof/band colour (body-coloured lids read as "lights off"); side views need an opaque cabin (open cabins let cards behind show through).
- Long cartoon contact shadows (sun tail) under vehicles read as potholes: tuck a plain contact ellipse under each auto.
- Riding shots among identical vehicles: clear the neighbours from the lens line or the hero is blocked.
- Fiction moods: a per-shot mood layer (lights.ts moodLight/moodLook) on top of day/golden carries the time-of-day arc.
- Slow warm narrators run long: check pace before the shot plan; ElevenLabs v4 has no speed setting (multilingual v2 has 0.7-1.2).
- Every wide view needs a filled horizon (city rows / haze); a flat plaza to the sky reads as desert or a tabletop.
