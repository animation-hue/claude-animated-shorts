## Cast (vehicles have faces: headlights = eyes, bumper/grille = mouth)
- **Raja** — small, battered, proud yellow-roof auto rickshaw (black body, yellow roof), dents and a cracked mirror.
- **The driver** — goggles, toothpick, speed-obsessed grin.
- **The passenger** — calm, clueless, blue shirt, a tie that flaps with the speed.
- **The red hatchback** — the love interest; calm, graceful.
- Big polished cars (SUV, silver sedan, white hatchback) and a black-and-yellow taxi — snooty, looking down on Raja.

## Voice-over (216 words → ≈58 s at 3.7 w/s; with the pauses on "Once. Softly." / "Slowly, this time." ≈ 62 s)

**HOOK**
> This is Raja. A small auto rickshaw with a yellow roof. Tonight, he fell in love, and everyone on Marine Drive could see it.

**SETTING**
> Mumbai. Evening rain on the sea road. The big, shiny cars look down on Raja. He overtakes them anyway.
> Which is bold, because autos aren't even allowed on Marine Drive. Nobody told Raja.

**CAST**
> His driver: goggles, a toothpick, and no brakes in his heart. His passenger: blue shirt, flapping tie, no idea what's going on.

**THE MOMENT**
> Then, in the slow traffic, a red hatchback glides past. For one second, their headlights meet.
> Raja's headlights go wide. His whole body shivers.

**ESCALATION**
> The driver notices. The toothpick stops. The grin grows.
> He thinks it's a race. Past the taxi. Past the SUV. Past the white hatchback. Past the silver sedan.
> The passenger stays perfectly calm. Only his tie knows how fast they're going.

**THE TURN**
> The rain stops. The light turns gold. At the last signal, the driver finally eases off, and Raja rolls to a stop, gently, right beside her.
> And Raja realizes she was never watching the race. She was watching that.

**ENDING**
> Side by side. Silence. The sea glowing behind them.
> Raja can't say a word. So he flickers his headlight. Once. Softly.
> She flickers back.
> Green light. They drive off together. Slowly, this time.
