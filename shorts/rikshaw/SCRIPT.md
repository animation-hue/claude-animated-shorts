## Voice-over (clean, for recording)
> At half past nine one morning, at a red light on Marine Drive, Raja the auto rickshaw fell in love.
> Her name was Rani. Pink roof. Jasmine on her mirror. Raja's headlights went wide. His little engine skipped a beat.
> Then the light turned green, and she was gone.
> Raja followed. Past the sea wall. Round the corner. Into the busiest roundabout in Mumbai, where every auto looks exactly the same. When he came out, she wasn't there.
> All day, he searched. A pink roof! A grandma. A pink roof! An umbrella. A pink roof… a pink roof that drove away.
> His headlights drooped a little lower every time.
> Then, at twenty past five, back on Marine Drive, he smelled jasmine. There she was, across the road.
> He didn't look left. He didn't look right. He only looked at her. Which is how he met the lamp post.
> One cracked headlight and a lot of tape later, Raja sat alone in a garage. He had never even said hello.
> Then the garage door filled with golden light, and someone honked, very softly. It was Rani. She had seen everything.
> She hung a jasmine flower on his wobbly mirror.
> Raja couldn't say a word. So he flickered his one good headlight. Once. Softly. She flickered back.
> And in the golden evening, two autos drove along Marine Drive. Slowly, this time.
